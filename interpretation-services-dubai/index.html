Types of interpretation

The right format for the room.

Tell us the setting and we recommend the format. Each one suits a different kind of session.

Simultaneous interpretation

Real-time interpreting for conferences, arbitration hearings, board meetings and multilingual events. Longer sessions are normally covered by two interpreters who take turns, and we tell you when that applies.

Consecutive interpretation

The speaker pauses and the interpreter renders each segment. Ideal for depositions, interviews, negotiations and site visits.

Court and sworn interpretation

Hearings and proceedings, with a sworn interpreter holding a valid UAE Ministry of Justice interpreter card where the authority requires one. We confirm the requirement before we confirm the booking.

Police and notary sessions

Witness statements, interviews and signings at police stations and notary offices, with the interpreter present on the day.

Whispered interpretation

Quiet, on-the-spot interpreting (chuchotage) for one or two listeners in a meeting or hearing, with no equipment needed.

Business negotiations and meetings

Contract talks, due-diligence sessions and client meetings, with terminology agreed with you in advance.

Sworn interpreters

When the authority asks for a sworn interpreter.

Some authorities ask for a sworn interpreter, sometimes one who holds a valid UAE Ministry of Justice interpreter card. Others accept any competent interpreter. The rule depends on the authority, the type of session and the language.

Tell us where the session takes place and what it is for. We tell you what we understand the requirement to be, and we confirm that the interpreter we propose meets it before you commit to the booking.

Check who appoints the interpreter. In some proceedings the court or the prosecution provides its own interpreter. If you are unsure, ask the authority or your lawyer first. We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. We help where you are free to bring an interpreter, or need one for the meetings, statements and signings around the case.

What to send us

  • Date, start time and expected duration.
  • Venue and the authority or organisation involved.
  • Type of session: hearing, statement, signing, negotiation or event.

FAQ

Interpretation in Dubai: common questions.

What types of interpretation do you provide?+

Simultaneous and consecutive interpretation, whispered interpretation (chuchotage), court and sworn interpretation, interpretation at police stations and notary offices, and interpretation for business negotiations and meetings. Tell us the setting and we recommend the format.

Which languages do you interpret between?+

Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Turkish, German and Urdu, in any direction. Arabic, English and French are the core of our work and the other languages are covered through our network of interpreters. Availability depends on the pair and the date, and other languages can be requested.

Can you provide a sworn interpreter with an interpreter card?+

Yes, where one is available for your language pair. Tell us the authority and the type of session. We say what we understand the requirement to be and confirm that the interpreter holds the credentials needed before you commit to the booking.

Can you interpret at court hearings and police stations?+

We arrange interpreters for hearings, depositions, witness statements and notary sessions. In some proceedings the court or the prosecution appoints its own interpreter, so check with the authority or your lawyer first. We do not give legal advice.

How far in advance should I book?+

As early as you can. Common pairs can sometimes be covered at short notice, while rarer pairs and sworn interpreters need more time. If it is urgent, message us on WhatsApp with the language pair, date, time and venue and we will tell you quickly whether we can cover it.

How much does interpretation cost?+

The price depends on the language pair, the type and length of the session, the venue and whether a sworn interpreter is needed. We quote each request individually once we have the details listed on this page.

Do you rent audiovisual equipment for conferences?+

Yes. We rent conference audiovisual equipment: interpretation booths and consoles, receivers and headsets, microphones and sound, and projectors and screens. You can rent it on its own or together with interpreters. Tell us the venue, dates, attendees and languages and we confirm availability and send a quote.

Is what is said during the session kept confidential?+

Yes. Every engagement is treated as confidential, and we will sign an NDA in writing if you need one.

Will the interpreter advise me or speak for me?+

No. An interpreter conveys what each person says, faithfully and completely, and does not advise, take sides or add comments. If you need legal advice, bring your lawyer, and we will interpret between you.

Can you also translate the documents that come with the session?+

Yes. We provide certified legal translation between Arabic, English and French by Ministry of Justice-accredited sworn translators, and can advise on attestation. See certified and sworn translation.
