Urgent does not mean uncertified.

A rush translation still goes through a second in-house translator before it is certified, and it is still signed and stamped by a Ministry of Justice-accredited sworn translator. The deadline changes how quickly we work, not whether the document is checked or certified. For what certification means and which steps are separate, see certified and sworn translation.

Rush work is quoted per document, so send the file and the deadline and we reply with what is possible. For routine work with a normal timeline, see legal translation.

Arabic, English and French

One team, every direction, no handoffs.

When time is short, splitting a file between vendors for each language is the slowest option. One team covers all six directions, with one glossary across the whole file.

Arabic → EnglishEnglish → ArabicArabic → FrenchFrench → ArabicEnglish → FrenchFrench → English

Legal terms rarely map one to one between Arabic, English and French. A notary public in the UAE (كاتب العدل) and a French notaire, for example, are not the same office. Under a deadline that is exactly the kind of difference a generalist misses, so the team that handles your rush job is the team that handles these three languages every day.

FAQ

Urgent legal translation: common questions.

How fast is your rush translation?+

Our rush service guarantees 24-hour turnaround on request, with a dedicated project manager. Tell us your deadline when you send the document.

Is a rush translation still certified?+

Yes. It is prepared and signed and stamped by Ministry of Justice-accredited sworn translators, and reviewed by a second in-house translator, like every legal document we deliver.

Can you do urgent work between Arabic, English and French?+

Yes. One team covers all six directions between the three languages, so an urgent file does not have to be split between vendors.
