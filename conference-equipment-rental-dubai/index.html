How interpretation equipment works

One speaker, many languages.

In simultaneous interpretation the audience hears the speaker in their own language almost in real time.

Speaker's microphone→ Interpreter in the booth→ One channel per language→ Delegate's receiver and headset

The interpreter hears the speaker through headphones, interprets into a microphone, and the delegates choose the channel for their language on a receiver. Each language you want to offer needs its own channel, so the number of languages decides how much equipment and how many interpreters the event needs. Tell us the languages and the number of attendees and we size it for you.

Can I rent the equipment without interpreters?+

Each language you offer needs its own channel for delegates and its own interpreters. Tell us the languages and the number of attendees and we confirm the setup the event needs.

Can you also supply the interpreters?+
